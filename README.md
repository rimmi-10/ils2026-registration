# IEEE WIE ILS 2026 -- National Hackathon

Registration, submission, screening, and judging system for the IEEE WIE ILS 2026 National Hackathon. Hosted via GitHub Pages.

## Portals

| Portal | Link | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Registration | [index.html](https://rimmi-10.github.io/ils2026-registration/index.html) | Participants -- team registration |
| Round 1 Submission | [submit-round1.html](https://rimmi-10.github.io/ils2026-registration/submit-round1.html) | Participants -- Idea/Solution Document upload |
| Round 2 Submission | [submit-round2.html](https://rimmi-10.github.io/ils2026-registration/submit-round2.html) | Shortlisted participants -- track status and deck upload |
| Screening Portal | [screen.html](https://rimmi-10.github.io/ils2026-registration/screen.html) | Screeners -- Round 1 pass/reject review |
| Judge Portal | [judge.html](https://rimmi-10.github.io/ils2026-registration/judge.html) | Judges -- scoring assigned teams |
| Admin Panel | [admin.html](https://rimmi-10.github.io/ils2026-registration/admin.html) | Organizers -- full management dashboard |

## Process

1. Teams register via the Registration portal.
2. Teams submit their Round 1 Idea/Solution Document.
3. Every submission is reviewed by 2 independent, randomly assigned Screeners (pass/reject on 4 gates).
4. Organizers resolve any screener disagreements and mark each team Shortlisted or Rejected.
5. Shortlisted teams are randomly assigned to 3 Judges each (4 teams per judge) for scored evaluation.
6. Track-wise rankings (by judge average) feed Round 2 selection.
7. Shortlisted teams track their status and submit their Round 2 deck via the Round 2 Submission portal.
